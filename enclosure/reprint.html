Front plate + clamps reprint

The v1 print's OLED window is ~3-4 mm off toward the pin edge, and the clamp bars land on SMD parts. One step at a time; values in mm.

What to measure (steps 1-2)

Front of the OLED, pins up, all pixels on (spare ESP32). Measure only to the glow: not the dark edge round it, not the glass, not the connection strip.

  1. Display running, pins up. From the board's pin-side edge down to the top of the lit picture (A). Recorded: 5.27 (to the glow, all pixels on)

  2. Same edge, to the bottom of the lit picture (B). Recorded: 16.10 (glow 10.83 tall)

  3. Back side, pins up, as in your photo. The clamps will press on a small ring round each of the 4 holes. For each hole: from the edge of the hole, the shortest distance to the nearest thing that sticks up (part, solder pad, header plastic, flex). Silkscreen text doesn't count.

    E1 top-left hole, E2 top-right hole (the round GND/VCC pads are near), E3 bottom-left hole, E4 bottom-right hole (the flex is near).

  4. A, B and the free corners.

  5. oled_lit_top = 5.27, height 10.83. The measurements are rough: window 1 mm bigger than the glow on every side (was 0.5), so ±1 still shows the whole picture.

  6. Relieved over the SMD band.
